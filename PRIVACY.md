## Purpose and stored data

Sewing Planner manages your sewing projects. It stores the project name, material, color, storage location, notes, one optional photo and project status, together with the text, order and completion marks of your work steps. These data, internal record identifiers and your language preference are stored locally in your computer's user profile by default. Text and photos may contain personal information. Processing serves the management and search functions you request.

## Local processing

The app requires no account or server. It does not send your data or language preference to the developer or other recipients. It contains no advertising, telemetry, usage analytics or built-in cloud synchronization. The app does not itself encrypt its local data. Protection also depends on your user account, access permissions and any disk encryption you have enabled.

## Photos and backups

The app reads only the JPEG or PNG image you select and stores a copy in its local database. It does not modify or delete the original file. Existing image metadata may remain in the stored copy. If you back up the data folder or store it in a synchronized folder or network location, those services may process data outside the app. Their settings and privacy terms apply. The app does not control these services.

## Retention and deletion

Data remain stored until you delete entries or remove the local app data. You can view, edit and delete entries in the app. To remove all local data, including photos and the language preference, close the app and remove its data folder. Storage locations are described in the included instructions. Original photos and backups must be deleted separately. Deleting an entry does not guarantee secure erasure of previous file contents. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to remove all app data and external copies.

## Contact and support

If you voluntarily send us an email, we process your email address, message and attachments to handle your request. Your locally stored collection is not attached automatically. We retain correspondence only as long as necessary to handle the request and meet statutory retention obligations.

## Your rights and other providers

Where applicable, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Use the privacy contact above to exercise these rights. The developer has no access to collections stored only on your computer. When you obtain or update the app through Microsoft Store or GitHub, these providers may process data independently under their own privacy terms. This policy describes processing by Sewing Planner and will be updated when that processing changes.

## Optimization of new photos

Newly selected or replaced photos are saved as a copy with a maximum edge of 1600 pixels, preserving the aspect ratio. Smaller images are not enlarged. Opaque images are saved as JPEG at quality 85%, transparent images as PNG. EXIF orientation is applied to the pixels. Source metadata such as camera and location information is not copied. The original file, existing stored photos and photos restored from JSON are unchanged.

## Collection export (including project status and checklist steps)

You can export all saved entries, selected color values and photos to a JSON file at a location you choose. The stored photo copies are included in full, with any metadata they still contain. The file is not encrypted. The app does not upload or send it. If you save it in a cloud or network folder, that provider may process it. Sharing and deleting exported copies is your responsibility.

## Collection import (including project status and checklist steps)

The app reads the JSON export you select and adds its entries, color values and photos to your local collection. Existing entries are preserved. Identical copies already present are skipped. The source file is not changed or transmitted. Imported photos retain any metadata contained in the export.

## Photo selection folder

The app remembers the folder of your last selected photo locally so that the next file selection starts there, including after an app restart. Only the folder path is stored in the app data folder. It is not included in collection exports or sent anywhere.
